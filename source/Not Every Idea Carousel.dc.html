What does a RACG project look like?
Helping Aussies doing it tough. Here are six simple points to keep in mind before you apply.
Swipe for the 6 points →
2 · Points 1–2
RACG projects
01–02 / 06
1
Applications come through Rotary
Your Rotary Club or District can submit an online application.
2
Help goes to people
The project gives direct relief to people in need. It isn’t a general donation to an organisation, even one that supports people in need.
3 · Points 3–4
RACG projects
03–04 / 06
3
It meets a specific need
Funding can go straight to a person or a small group. It can also go to an organisation for a piece of equipment that meets the needs of a disadvantaged group.
4
It supports those who need it most
The focus is on disadvantaged people, or a small disadvantaged part of the community, rather than a benefit for everyone.
4 · Points 5–6
RACG projects
05–06 / 06
5
It moves people
A good RACG project is one the community can connect with and want to get behind.
6
It names who and what
Say who the project is for and what they need. The clearer it is, the easier it is for others to understand and care.

2 · Criteria 1–2
RACG criteria
01–02 / 06
1
RACG online Project Applications may be submitted by a Rotary Club or a Rotary District.
2
RACG Projects must provide specific, direct relief to people in need and not be simply a general funding donation to an organisation even though that organisation may provide assistance to people in need.
3 · Criteria 3–4
RACG criteria
03–04 / 06
3
The funding must satisfy a specific need and may be provided directly to a person or a small group of people. Alternatively, the funding may be to an organisation to provide a piece of equipment that will satisfy the needs of a disadvantaged section of the community.
4
The funding must satisfy the needs of disadvantaged people or a small disadvantaged section of the community and not be simply to provide a benefit to the whole community.
4 · Criteria 5–6
RACG criteria
05–06 / 06
5
The project should be such as to arouse the compassion of the community.
6
The project should define and target the disadvantage or need of the people or group of people for whom it is intended.
